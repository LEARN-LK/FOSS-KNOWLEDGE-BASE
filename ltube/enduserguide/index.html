    LEARN Tube (LTube) User Guide

    Welcome to the official user guide for LEARN Tube (LTube) (ltube.ac.lk), the open-source media streaming and publishing platform provided by the Lanka Education and Research Network (LEARN) for Sri Lankan state universities and higher education institutions.

    Table of Contents

    1. Login & Authentication.
    2. 1.1 Workspace Request for Faculties & Departments
    3. Edit Profile.
    4. Media Upload & Management.
    5. Edit Video Details.
    6. Publishing the Video.
    7. Creating & Managing Playlists.
    8. Reference & Support.

    1. Login & Authentication

    To start using LTube to upload or manage content, you must sign in using your university credentials via the LEARN Identity Access Federation (LIAF).

    1. Open your web browser and navigate to ltube.ac.lk.

    2. Click the Sign In button at the top-right corner of the page.

    3. Select your home institution from the LIAF directory.

    4. Enter your institutional username and password (eg: eduID).

    2. Workspace Request for Faculties & Departments

    If your faculty, department, or unit requires a dedicated workspace to organize content and manage team permissions:

    1. Send an official request email including:
    2. Faculty / Department Name
    3. Primary Contact Person & Institutional Email
    4. List of Designated Channel Admins
    5. Address the email to the LEARN FOSS team:
    foss-team@learn.ac.lk

    3. Edit Profile

    Customize your profile details to ensure students can identify official course material.

    1. Click your avatar in the top-right menu and select Settings (or Edit Profile).

    2. Update your profile information.

    • Display Name: is not editable in LTube
    • Profile Picture: Upload a headshot or department logo (.png / .jpg).

    • Bio: Add a short summary of your modules or department focus.

    • Click Update Profile.

    4. Media Upload & Management

    LTube supports standard media file formats, including .mp4, .mov, .mkv, .avi, and .mp3.

    1. Click the Upload Media icon in the top navigation menu.

    2. Drag and drop your media file into the upload zone, or click Browse Files to choose a file from your device.

    1. Keep the browser open until the raw upload finishes then click on the View media.

    5. Edit Video Details

    1. Click on Edit Media.

    1. Title: Follow a consistent naming scheme (e.g., Tutorial 18 – Moodle Adding Users and Enrolment).
    2. Description: Add a lecture summary, topic timestamps, and links to supplementary course material.
    3. Category: Choose your respective Faculty and Department from the drop-down list.
    4. Tags: Add relevant search tags separated by commas (e.g., Moodle, Users, Enrolment).

    5. Thumbnail: upload a custom image (1280x720 recommended).

    6. then move on the Publish tab

    6. Publishing the Video

    When uploading a video, the configuration workflow is split across tabs (Metadata [1] - Trim [2] - Publish [3] ):

    1. Complete all necessary video details in the 1st tab (Metadata) [1]

    2. Once the metadata is filled out, click on the 3rd tab (Publish) [3] to configure the other options.

    3. Please select the relevant category for the video,

    4. Set the appropriate access level before making your content live:

    5. Public: Searchable on LTube and visible on public feeds and category pages.

    6. Unlisted: Hidden from search feeds; accessible only to users with the direct video URL or embedded LMS link.

    7. Private: Accessible only to you and designated workspace managers.

      • Choose Public, Unlisted, or Private in the Privacy / Visibility section.

    8. Review the details and click Publish.

    7. Creating & Managing Playlists

    Creating a Playlist

    1. Select the video you want to add to a playlist, then click on the Save button.

    2. If you don't have playlists, you can create them directly from the same popup window.

    3. Create a playlist according to your specific requirements.

    4. Add the video to the relevant playlist by marking the checkbox next to it.

    5. Click Save to confirm and add the video to your selected playlist.

    6. Now you can find your playlist on slidebar
