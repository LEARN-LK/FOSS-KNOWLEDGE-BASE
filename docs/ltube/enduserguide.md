## 1. Login & Authentication

To start using LTube to upload or manage content, you must sign in using your university credentials via the **LEARN Identity Access Federation (LIAF)**.

1. Open your web browser and navigate to [ltube.ac.lk](https://ltube.ac.lk).
2. Click the **Sign In** button at the top-right corner of the page.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/sign-in.png">

3. Select your home institution from the LIAF directory.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/LIAF.png" style="
    width: 60%; ">
4. Enter your institutional username and password (eg: `eduID`).
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/login.png" style="
    width: 60%; ">

## 2. Workspace Request for Faculties & Departments

If your faculty, department, or unit requires a dedicated workspace to organize content and manage team permissions:

1. Send an official request email including:
   - **Faculty / Department Name**
   - **Primary Contact Person & Institutional Email**
   - **List of Designated Channel Admins**
2. Address the email to the LEARN FOSS team:

```text
foss-team@learn.ac.lk 
```

## 3. Edit Profile

Customize your profile details to ensure students can identify official course material.

1. Click your avatar in the top-right menu and select **Settings** (or **Edit Profile**).
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/profile.png" style="
    width: 60%; ">

2. Update your profile information.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/edit-profile.png" style="
    width: 60%; ">

* **Display Name:**  is not editable in LTube
* **Profile Picture:** Upload a headshot or department logo (`.png` / `.jpg`).
* **Bio:** Add a short summary of your modules or department focus.

3. Click **Update Profile**.

## 4. Media Upload & Management

LTube supports standard media file formats, including `.mp4`, `.mov`, `.mkv`, `.avi`, and `.mp3`.

1. Click the **Upload Media** icon in the top navigation menu.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/uplord-btn.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

2. Drag and drop your media file into the upload zone, or click **Browse Files** to choose a file from your device.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/browse-btn.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

3. Keep the browser open until the raw upload finishes then click on the View media.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/view-media.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

## 5. Edit Video Details

1. Click on Edit Media. 

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/edit-media-btn.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

2. **Title:** Follow a consistent naming scheme (e.g., `Tutorial 18 – Moodle Adding Users and Enrolment`).
3. **Description:** Add a lecture summary, topic timestamps, and links to supplementary course material.
4. **Category:** Choose your respective **Faculty** and **Department** from the drop-down list.
5. **Tags:** Add relevant search tags separated by commas (e.g., `Moodle, Users, Enrolment`).
6. **Thumbnail:**  upload a custom image (`1280x720` recommended).
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/add-metadata.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

7. then move on the Publish tab

## 6. Publishing the Video

1. Please select the relevant category for the video,

2. Set the appropriate access level before making your content live:

* **Public:** Searchable on LTube and visible on public feeds and category pages.
* **Unlisted:** Hidden from search feeds; accessible only to users with the direct video URL or embedded LMS link.
* **Private:** Accessible only to you and designated workspace managers.
    
    - Choose **Public**, **Unlisted**, or **Private** in the **Privacy / Visibility** section.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/publish.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

3. Review the details and click **Publish**.

## 7. Creating & Managing Playlists

 <!-- Playlists organize separate lecture recordings into sequential course series. -->

### Creating a Playlist

1. Select the video you want to add to a playlist, then click on the Save button.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/save-btn.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

2. If you don't have playlists, you can create them directly from the same popup window.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/new-playlist.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

3. Create a playlist according to your specific requirements.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/create-new-playlist.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

4. Add the video to the relevant playlist by marking the checkbox next to it.
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/save-video.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">

5. Click Save to confirm and add the video to your selected playlist.
6. Now you can find your playlist on slidebar
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LEARN-LK/LTUBE/main/images/kb/view-playlist.png" style="
    width: 75%; ">
